# Milestone 134 — Journal fit for the revised NKM manuscript

Completed: 2026-10-11. Scope: read-only manuscript assessment and journal advice.

Read `docs/paper/paper.tex` and its current numerical macros. The revised
title is *Finite-length and vertical effects in nonlinear-kicker injection into
the Korea Light Source*. It now presents an exact filament-field model, a
finite-length mechanism, physical vertical kicks, threshold-defined operating
windows, alignment transparency and parameterized error sensitivities. This
assessment concerns the revised paper, not the earlier technical-framework draft.

## Recommendation

1. **Physical Review Accelerators and Beams (PRAB), full research article:** best
   match for the current beam-dynamics and magnet-model physics emphasis. Lead
   with the finite-length mechanism, model validity criterion and design implications
   beyond the selected lattice. Separate correcting an input-map sign error from
   a novel physical result. The official scope includes single-particle dynamics,
   magnet calculations and accelerator design studies. PRAB currently provides
   open access without author publication charges.
2. **Journal of Instrumentation (JINST), regular research article:** strong
   alternative for the NKM injection-system model, performance and tolerance
   allocation. Its scope explicitly includes accelerator theory, modelling and
   simulation. The existing template does not determine the best scientific venue.
3. **Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research A (NIM A):** suitable
   alternative if the article emphasizes the practical magnet/injection-system
   design and performance requirements. Its publisher describes a scientific
   instrument design, development and performance scope.

This is an editorial-fit judgment, not a new referee acceptance decision. The
ideal-lattice and assumed-error limits must remain explicit; the assessment does
not certify all revised physics or require experimental work solely to qualify
for a simulation article. No submission or external communication was performed.
