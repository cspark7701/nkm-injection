# Cover letter (draft for author review — not sent)

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript "Capture window and robustness of nonlinear-kicker injection into a 4 GeV
storage ring with a small dynamic aperture" for publication as a regular paper in the Journal of
Instrumentation.

Single nonlinear or multipole injection kickers make top-up injection transparent for the stored beam,
but their kick varies across the injected bunch, and fourth-generation lattices leave little dynamic
aperture to absorb that variation. The manuscript quantifies this trade-off for a RADIA-modelled
wire-based kicker in a 4 GeV ring whose on-momentum dynamic aperture at the injection point is about
−12 to +13 mm, using a transfer line tracked particle by particle from the booster and native nonlinear
ring tracking with paired, calibrated control kickers.

The principal findings are: (i) a linear one-turn-map ring model overestimates capture by tens of
percentage points and is not adequate for such designs; (ii) capture is highest when the incoming
angle is cancelled near the kick-map extremum (91 % over 500 turns, versus 95 % for an equally strong
uniform kick), with an angular window of about ±0.5 mrad; (iii) the aligned kicker leaves the stored
beam essentially undisturbed, and its transparency is limited by kicker-to-orbit alignment at the
0.1–0.2 mm level because the field is cubic near the axis; (iv) under a combined error budget the mean
capture falls to about 38 %, limited by the injected-orbit jitter transmitted from booster extraction,
which identifies the tolerance that must be met before the window can be used.

The closest previous work comprises the KEK-PF pulsed-sextupole injection, the BESSY II non-linear
kicker and the MAX IV/SOLEIL multipole injection kicker; Liu et al. identified kick nonlinearity and
injected-beam size as the efficiency limit. The present article adds a quantitative capture window,
transparency-versus-alignment relation and combined-error robustness for a specific design with a small
dynamic aperture. The study is simulation-only and does not claim experimental validation. All figures
and numbers are generated from hashed simulation artifacts; the code is publicly available.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. Generative AI assistance used in its preparation is
declared in the acknowledgments.

Sincerely,
