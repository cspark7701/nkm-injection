# Cover letter (draft for author review — not sent)

Dear Editors of Physical Review Accelerators and Beams,

Please consider “Finite-length and vertical effects in nonlinear-kicker injection
into a Korea Light Source design lattice” for publication as a Research Article in Physical Review Accelerators and Beams.

The manuscript studies a preliminary 0.525 m four-loop nonlinear kicker in the
supplied 4 GeV KLS design lattice. It compares exact filament-field tracking,
thin and sliced vector maps, and an independent adaptive trajectory solver. A
sagitta-to-kick-width ratio organizes finite-length differences over two related
wire families. The vertical-field sign is treated as an input consistency check,
not as a limitation inherent to vector kick maps.

The revised capture studies use the design booster beam, an explicitly declared
chamber and ten corrected-error rings. They distinguish nominal finite-turn
survival from a joint machine/shot distribution, report paired jitter differences
and quantify reduced capture-based tuning on held-out particles. Two-plane wire
and current tolerances distinguish ideal centroid cancellation from finite-beam
moment changes. Pulse, chamber and septum estimates restrict the interpretation
to a simulation study, without a commissioned-efficiency or system-transparency
claim.

A point-by-point response to two rounds of pre-submission assessment documents
the changes. The numerical
claims are linked to hashed artifacts and a frozen local evidence bundle prepared
as a referee attachment, including committed source and verified figure/macro
rebuild instructions. The code repository is
<https://github.com/cspark7701/nkm-injection>; the ignored simulation outputs are
provided through the attachment rather than represented as repository contents.
Generative AI assistance is disclosed in the manuscript acknowledgments.

Sincerely,
