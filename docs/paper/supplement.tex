\section{Larger-emittance sensitivity case}
\label{sec:s-largebeam}

Before the design booster beam was supplied, the studies used a 100/10\,nm\,rad beam with rms energy spread 0.0011, the
septum aperture and no additional chamber profile. For \SurvN\ particles (seeds \SurvSeeds), \SurvShort\,\% survive
500 turns and \SurvLong\,\% survive \SurvTurns\ turns (95\,\% particle Wilson interval \SurvLongLo--\SurvLongHi\,\%);
the late loss is \SurvLateLossPct\ percentage points (Fig.~\ref{fig:s-accept}). A separate \BigN-particle sample gives
\BigCap\,\% (\BigLo--\BigHi\,\%) at 500 turns. In that case the paired vertical-kick diagnostic gives \AperDiagRef\,\%
with the NKM vertical kick and \AperDiagOpenNoVert\,\% when it is suppressed; a uniform 5\,mm vertical half-aperture
reduces capture with or without that kick (to \AperDiagXopen\ and \AperDiagNoVert\,\%), which supports a role for
transverse transfer in the ring without identifying a unique coupling resonance. These numbers belong to the
larger-emittance case and are not design-beam results.

\section{Statistical methods}
\label{sec:s-stats}

\emph{Particle intervals.} Capture of a fixed bunch is a binomial fraction; its 95\,\% interval is the Wilson score
interval over the pooled particles of the stated seeds. It describes particle sampling for fixed models, ring and
settings only.

\emph{Realization intervals.} Joint machine/shot ensembles are summarized by their mean, median, tenth percentile and
fraction of realizations at or above 80\,\%. Intervals of the mean are percentile bootstraps over joint realizations
(10\,000 resamples, seed 1729). Because each realization draws one of ten corrected rings with replacement, a second,
ring-cluster bootstrap resamples the ten ring identities and weights their realizations; together with the
leave-one-ring-out means it shows how much the result depends on the finite ring population.

\emph{Paired differences.} Comparisons between models, jitter levels or tuned and untuned settings use the same
particles and error draws; the paired difference is bootstrapped over realizations (10\,000 resamples, seed 1729).

\emph{Acceptable-loss test.} Whether a jitter level costs less than one percentage point is a one-sided
noninferiority question: the lower 95\,\% bootstrap endpoint of the paired difference must lie above $-1$ percentage
point. The one-point value is the declared analysis margin of the robustness analysis; it is not a design
specification, and an interval that crosses it is reported as inconclusive rather than as equivalence.

\emph{Associations.} Partial Spearman rank correlations control the other sampled error magnitudes and the ring
identity; two-sided probabilities from the asymptotic $t$ statistic are Holm-adjusted over the sampled-error
hypotheses. They describe associations within the sampled model, not causal error rankings.

\section{Procedures}
\label{sec:s-procedures}

\emph{Corrected rings.} Each of ten error draws (seeds 5501--5510) is commissioned in ten steps of increasing error
amplitude. BPM readings are referenced to the adjacent quadrupole centre with a 10\,$\mu$m rms residual; the orbit is
corrected with the 120 corrector pairs by truncated SVD (cutoff $10^{-3}$ of the largest singular value, $\pm1$\,mrad
bound) until the rms improves by less than 1\,\% per iteration. The $\beta$-beat and horizontal dispersion at the BPMs
are then fitted with the 344 individual quadrupole gradients (Gauss--Newton on a fixed forward-difference Jacobian,
SVD cutoff $10^{-2}$, changes bounded to 1\,\% per step, three accepted steps), followed by bounded Q11/Q12 tune
correction and a final orbit correction. No skew correctors exist; coupling is not corrected.

\emph{Operating point.} Candidates place $x_\mathrm{inj}$ at the 3$\sigma_x$ septum-clearance limit and 0.25, 0.5 and
1.0\,mm further out, with $S=0.80$--1.00 and the inner matched root of the 3-D magnet; each is scored by the tenth
percentile, then the mean, of capture over the corrected rings (100 particles per ring, no injection errors), for
2\,mm and 1\,mm blades. The selected points are validated on fresh 2$\times$500-particle bunches; the paired dipole
control is calibrated to the 3-D centroid response at each validated point.

\emph{Window.} Capture is evaluated on a fixed 0.2\,mm/mrad grid of septum-exit offsets about the selected point,
extended while a boundary remains above 80\,\% (maximum offset 3), with crossing cells refined once to 0.1 and linearly
interpolated. Two 500-particle bunches (seeds 1001/1002) per value.

\emph{Tuning.} For each of 50 static machines, capture of 100 fresh training particles (all shot errors zero) is
maximized over $x_\mathrm{inj}$ (bounded above by the clearance limit, 1.5\,mm range), an angle trim of
$\pm0.5$\,mrad about the re-matched angle of the current $(x_\mathrm{inj},S)$, and $S$ (0.75--1.05), by repeated
strict-improvement coordinate sweeps (steps 0.25\,mm, 0.1\,mrad, 0.025; at most four sweeps). Held-out validation
restores the joint shot errors on the paired 200-particle samples at zero and quarter booster jitter. The fraction of
machines whose optimum lies on a bound is reported.
