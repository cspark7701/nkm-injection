# Article scope after Reviewer1 round2

The article compares reference, refined low-beta and nominal direct-clearance settings at a common parametric exit target. An immutable entrance caps the full minimum; hypothetical horizontal m2 and vertical m3 restrictions expose competing size, dispersion and centroid response. New tests use independently drawn machines after frozen nominal design selection.

Original R1 evidence is preserved, including the unresolved original mean-clearance comparison. The new baseline also remains unresolved; direct nominal clearance gains appear in both selected restricted cases, while low beta helps vertically and hurts horizontally. Greater mean clearance does not imply fewer losses. All statistical intervals are pointwise and conditional on modelling assumptions. No robust optimization, global optimum, operating tolerance, continuous-chamber certification, storage-ring capture or NKM transparency is established.

The principal reproduction and limitation records are round2_reproduction.md and round2_scope.md. All prior reports remain historical assessments of their identified versions. Task completion and compilation do not imply journal acceptance. The companion NKM study shares upstream BTS assets, not the new aperture-regime evidence.
