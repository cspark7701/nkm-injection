# Selected corrected ring scenario

The scenario applies 30 µm rms correlated girder x/y offsets, 30 µm independent magnet offsets, 100 µrad rolls, 0.05% relative quadrupole/combined-bend gradients, 0.1% sextupole strengths and 1e-4 relative skew-quadrupole components. These are sensitivity assumptions, not KLS construction data. All 140 GirderStart spans and magnetic elements in the supplied lattice are used. Seeds 5501–5510 are fixed; per-magnet errors and final lattice files are saved.

The numerical coupled closed-orbit response uses every BPM marker and the 120 HVC correctors, in both planes. A 1 µrad central difference constructs the response; SVD retains singular values above 0.001 of the largest. Corrector angles are bounded by ±1 mrad. A ten-step error ramp commissions the same final realization without discarding failed seeds. Q11/Q12 tune correction uses bounded additive gradient steps (at most 0.002 m^-2), damped Newton iteration and line-search rejection of unstable trials. The target is the ideal fractional tune, with a 1e-5 residual criterion. Every step and termination is recorded.

An initial attempt to require 20 µm rms at every BPM was not attained: the available correction fit settled near 65–80 µm horizontally and 43–55 µm vertically, with unsaturated correctors. Retaining all response modes did not remove that floor. This trial remains in `d5_corrected_ring`; it is not evidence that a KLS operating specification is met. The selected scenario in `d5_selected_scenario` declares 100 µm rms acceptance, half the assumed 200 µm orbit-error scale in the capture budget. Its actual residuals and tunes, rather than a claim of an as-built corrected machine, define the scenario.

Native 6-D dynamic aperture uses one particle per 0.5 mm horizontal launch over ±16 mm, 500 turns, zero transverse angles and momentum offset relative to the corrected 6-D orbit, with RF and radiation. The only aperture here is the study septum; D6 adds the chamber. The observation point is the NKM centre. The full survival grid is saved per seed, so isolated stable islands are not silently reported as contiguous acceptance.

Reproduction: `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python scripts/run_corrected_ring_scenario.py --study-config results/journal_campaign_03/operating_point_design_beam.json --output-dir results/journal_campaign_03/<new> --orbit-tolerance-um 100`. All ten selected seeds pass the stated scenario gates. Corrected lattices and errors under `results/journal_campaign_03/d5_selected_scenario/` are inputs to D6–D8.
